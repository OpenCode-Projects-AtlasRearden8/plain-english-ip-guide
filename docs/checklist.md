# Pre-Publish Asset Checklist

Run this before you hit **publish** on anything — a blog post, a cover
reveal, a book, a trailer, a paid course. Each item is a yes/no question; the
honest answers take minutes and prevent the takedowns.

Each of these topics is explained in its guide (linked below where useful).

## Everything you publish

- [ ] I made everything in this post/content myself, **or** every borrowed
      piece is logged in the
      [permission log](templates/permission-log.csv) with source + license.
- [ ] No image came from a Google/Pinterest search without a license on the
      *source* page
      ([why](06-accidental-reuse.md#the-traps-most-common-first)).
- [ ] No "free for personal use" font, graphic, or music is being used for my
      LLC ([decode](04-public-domain-and-creative-commons.md)).
- [ ] Every quoted lyric, book paragraph, or screenshot is (a) brief, (b)
      serving my actual commentary/criticism, and (c) credited
      ([fair-use myths](03-fair-use-and-quote-myths.md)).
- [ ] Anything built from a "free template" had its embedded fonts/images
      checked and replaced if unlicensed.
- [ ] My cover art and logo either are mine, or I have the license/contract
      in writing ([contracts with
      freelancers](06-accidental-reuse.md#the-traps-most-common-first)).

## If I registered or plan to register

- [ ] The work is registered (or scheduled to be) within **3 months of first
      publication** if it has real commercial value
      ([what registration buys](02-what-registration-buys.md)).
- [ ] The registration includes only material I own or have permission to
      include.
- [ ] My series name/brand passed the one-evening knockout searches before
      launch ([trademark decode](07-trademark-basics-for-series-names.md)).
- [ ] I use **™** (not **®**) on unregistered brand names.

## The two-minute walk-through

1. List every asset in this content that you didn't make — image, font,
   music, clip, screenshot, phrase.
2. For each, find the row in the permission log. Missing? Locate the source
   page *now*, and either license, replace, or drop it.
3. Check each quote against
   [the fair-use four factors](03-fair-use-and-quote-myths.md#what-fair-use-really-is)
   — briefly and honestly.
4. Publish.

> **Education, never legal advice.** This checklist is a documentation habit,
> not a legal opinion. When real money or real disputes are involved, talk to
> a real IP attorney.
