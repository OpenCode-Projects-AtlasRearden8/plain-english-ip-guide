Plain-English IP Guide for Small Creators

Pre-Publish Asset Checklist

Run this before you hit publish on anything — a blog post, a cover reveal, a book, a trailer, a paid course. Each item is a yes/no question; the honest answers take minutes and prevent the takedowns.

Each of these topics is explained in its guide (linked below where useful).

Everything you publish

  • [ ] I made everything in this post/content myself, or every borrowed piece is logged in the permission log with source + license.
  • [ ] No image came from a Google/Pinterest search without a license on the source page (why).
  • [ ] No "free for personal use" font, graphic, or music is being used for my LLC (decode).
  • [ ] Every quoted lyric, book paragraph, or screenshot is (a) brief, (b) serving my actual commentary/criticism, and (c) credited (fair-use myths).
  • [ ] Anything built from a "free template" had its embedded fonts/images checked and replaced if unlicensed.
  • [ ] My cover art and logo either are mine, or I have the license/contract in writing (contracts with freelancers).

If I registered or plan to register

  • [ ] The work is registered (or scheduled to be) within 3 months of first publication if it has real commercial value (what registration buys).
  • [ ] The registration includes only material I own or have permission to include.
  • [ ] My series name/brand passed the one-evening knockout searches before launch (trademark decode).
  • [ ] I use ™ (not ®) on unregistered brand names.

The two-minute walk-through

  1. List every asset in this content that you didn't make — image, font, music, clip, screenshot, phrase.
  2. For each, find the row in the permission log. Missing? Locate the source page now, and either license, replace, or drop it.
  3. Check each quote against the fair-use four factors — briefly and honestly.
  4. Publish.

Education, never legal advice. This checklist is a documentation habit, not a legal opinion. When real money or real disputes are involved, talk to a real IP attorney.
